# Cell-Cell Interaction Analysis: FOL2 → FOL1 → FOL3 Trajectory
## Lymphoid Structure Formation During DSS-Induced Colitis

====================================================================================================

### Major Transitions Along the Trajectory

#### 1. Complement System Repurposing

**FOL2:** C3 binds Itgax/Itgam on APCs (activate innate immunity)

**FOL3:** C3 binds Cd19/Cr2 on B cells (amplify adaptive immunity)

- Same ligand, different receptors and biological function
- Reflects transition from innate to adaptive immunity
- C3d-CR2 co-ligation amplifies BCR signaling 1000-10000 fold

#### 2. Cellular Dominance Shift

**FOL2:** Stromal cells (52%) > Immune cells

**FOL1:** B cells (57%) > Stromal cells

**FOL3:** GC B cells (57% Aicda+) dominate

#### 3. Interaction Complexity

**FOL2:** 2,599 interactions (28 cell types)

**FOL3:** 357 interactions (8 cell types)

- 86% reduction in interaction diversity
- From exploratory to specialized
- Mature structures are more focused

#### 4. Chemokine Function

**FOL2:** Recruitment (Cxcl16, Ccl20)

**FOL3:** Retention (Cxcl12, Cxcl13)

- From attracting cells to maintaining architecture

#### 5. Key Cell Type Transitions

**ILC3 → Tfh:** Innate to adaptive T cell help

**B cell 1/2 → Aicda+ B cells:** Naive to GC B cells

**FRC dominance → B cell dominance:** Stromal to lymphoid

## BIOLOGICAL INSIGHTS & INTERPRETATION

### 1. Tertiary Lymphoid Structure Formation

This trajectory recapitulates **de novo lymphoid organogenesis** in inflamed tissue:

**Stage 1 (FOL2):** Stromal activation and innate immune recruitment
- FRC and fibroblasts create inflammatory microenvironment
- ILC3-LTi cells act as lymphoid tissue inducers
- Chemokines establish initial gradients

**Stage 2 (FOL1):** Organized follicle formation
- B and T cells segregate into distinct zones
- Tfh cells provide cognate help to B cells
- Early germinal center reactions begin

**Stage 3 (FOL3):** Mature germinal centers
- Aicda+ B cells undergo somatic hypermutation
- Affinity-based selection produces high-affinity antibodies
- Plasma cells and memory B cells emerge

### 2. Inflammation-Associated Interactions

**Complement Activation (C3):**
- Persistent throughout trajectory
- Shifts from innate activation to adaptive amplification
- Fibroblast-derived C3 is critical at all stages

**Chemokine Networks:**
- Early: Cxcl16, Ccl20 (recruitment)
- Late: Cxcl12, Cxcl13 (retention and organization)
- Transition reflects maturation of lymphoid structure

**Adhesion Molecules:**
- Critical for cell-cell conjugate formation
- Tfh-B cell immune synapses require multiple adhesion pairs
- ICAM1-LFA1, Selectins maintain follicle architecture

### 3. Key Immune Cell Crosstalk

#### Fibroblast-Immune Cell Axis
**Persistent throughout trajectory:**
- C3 production (complement activation)
- Cxcl12 production (cell retention)
- BAFF/APRIL (B cell survival)
- Structural scaffold for lymphoid organization

#### ILC3-DC Axis (FOL2)
**Early lymphoid induction:**
- Thy1-Itgb2 bidirectional signaling
- Cxcl16-Cxcr6 recruitment
- Critical for initiating TLS formation

#### Tfh-B Cell Axis (FOL1/FOL3)
**Germinal center maintenance:**
- CD40L-CD40 (B cell activation)
- ICOS-ICOSL (Tfh function)
- IL-21 signaling (plasma cell differentiation)
- Cxcl13-Cxcr5 (positioning)

### 4. Unexpected Findings

**1. Dramatic Complement System Repurposing:**
- Same molecule (C3) serves different functions at different stages
- FOL2: C3-Itgax/Itgam activates APCs
- FOL3: C3-Cd19/Cr2 amplifies BCR signaling
- Suggests microenvironment actively shapes immune responses

**2. Persistent Fibroblast Support:**
- Despite B cell dominance in FOL3, fibroblasts remain critical
- Provide C3, Cxcl12, BAFF throughout trajectory
- Stromal cells are not just passive scaffolds

**3. ILC3 Transience:**
- ILC3-LTi cells critical in FOL2 (1.3%)
- Nearly absent in FOL3 (0.1%)
- Suggests ILC3 are initiators, not maintainers of TLS

**4. Reduced Complexity in Maturation:**
- Mature GC (FOL3) has 86% fewer interactions than early stage (FOL2)
- Specialization reduces diversity
- Functional maturity = focused interactions

**5. Btla-Cd79a Interactions:**
- Prominent in FOL3 GC B cells
- Co-inhibitory checkpoint in germinal center
- May prevent excessive B cell activation
- Potential therapeutic target

## THERAPEUTIC IMPLICATIONS

### 1. Targeting TLS Formation (Early Intervention)

**Target:** FOL2-like neighborhoods

**Strategies:**
- **Anti-C3 therapy:** Block fibroblast-derived complement
- **Cxcl16/Cxcr6 blockade:** Prevent ILC3 recruitment
- **ILC3 depletion:** Remove lymphoid tissue inducers
- **Fibroblast targeting:** Disrupt stromal support network

**Rationale:**
- Prevent TLS formation before organized structures emerge
- May reduce chronic inflammation and autoimmunity
- Relevant for IBD, rheumatoid arthritis, Sjögren's syndrome

### 2. Disrupting Germinal Centers (Late Intervention)

**Target:** FOL3-like neighborhoods

**Strategies:**
- **Anti-CD40L (Tfh blockade):** Disrupt Tfh-B cell interactions
- **ICOS blockade:** Inhibit Tfh function
- **Cxcl12 neutralization:** Disrupt GC retention
- **Btla agonists:** Enhance co-inhibitory signals
- **C3-CR2 blockade:** Reduce BCR amplification

**Rationale:**
- Disrupt established germinal centers
- Reduce autoantibody production
- Relevant for lupus, Sjögren's, chronic IBD

### 3. Potential Drug Targets

**High Priority:**
1. **C3/C3aR/C5aR:** Complement inhibitors (eculizumab, ravulizumab)
2. **CXCL12/CXCR4:** AMD3100 (plerixafor) - FDA approved
3. **CD40L:** Dapirolizumab, frexalimab (in trials)
4. **ICOS:** KY1044, GSK3359609 (in trials)
5. **BAFF/APRIL:** Belimumab (FDA approved for lupus)

**Emerging Targets:**
6. **CXCL16/CXCR6:** No approved drugs yet
7. **Btla:** Agonist antibodies (preclinical)
8. **IL-21/IL-21R:** NNC0114-0006 (in trials)
9. **Fibroblast activation protein (FAP):** Sibrotuzumab

### 4. Biomarkers for Disease Monitoring

**Early TLS Formation (FOL2-like):**
- Elevated C3 in tissue
- Cxcl16+ macrophages
- ILC3 infiltration
- FRC expansion

**Mature GC (FOL3-like):**
- Aicda+ B cells in tissue
- Tfh:B cell ratios
- Cxcl12 expression levels
- Plasma cell emergence

### 5. Clinical Implications

**For IBD:**
- TLS formation correlates with disease severity
- FOL2-like neighborhoods may predict flares
- FOL3-like neighborhoods indicate chronic inflammation
- Targeting specific trajectory stages may improve outcomes

**For Other Autoimmune Diseases:**
- TLS are common in RA, Sjögren's, lupus, MS
- Similar interaction patterns likely present
- Therapeutic strategies may be generalizable

## SUMMARY OF KEY FINDINGS

### Trajectory Overview

The FOL2 → FOL1 → FOL3 trajectory represents **de novo tertiary lymphoid structure formation** during DSS-induced colitis, progressing from:

1. **Stromal activation and innate recruitment** (FOL2, DSS3)
2. **Organized B/T cell follicles** (FOL1, DSS9)
3. **Mature germinal centers with antibody production** (FOL3, DSS21)

### Critical Cell-Cell Interactions

**FOL2 (Early):**
- Fibroblast → APC (C3-Itgax/Itgam): Innate activation
- Macrophage → ILC3 (Cxcl16-Cxcr6): Lymphoid induction
- B cell → B cell (Cxcl13-Cxcr5): Early aggregation
- ILC3 ↔ DC (Thy1-Itgb2): Tissue organization

**FOL3 (Late):**
- Fibroblast → B cell (C3-Cd19/Cr2): BCR amplification
- B cell → Tfh (Icosl-Icos, Cxcl13-Cxcr5): GC maintenance
- Tfh → B cell (CD40L-CD40, IL-21): B cell selection
- Fibroblast → Tfh/B cell (Cxcl12-Cxcr4/Cd4): Retention

### Most Surprising Findings

1. **Complement system repurposing:** C3 shifts from activating APCs to amplifying BCR signaling
2. **Persistent fibroblast support:** Stromal cells remain critical despite lymphocyte dominance
3. **ILC3 transience:** Critical for initiation but not maintenance
4. **Reduced complexity in maturation:** Mature GC have 86% fewer interactions
5. **Btla-Cd79a checkpoint:** Prominent in GC, potential therapeutic target

### Therapeutic Opportunities

**Early Intervention (FOL2-like):**
- Target C3, Cxcl16, ILC3, fibroblasts
- Prevent TLS formation

**Late Intervention (FOL3-like):**
- Target CD40L, ICOS, Cxcl12, Btla
- Disrupt established GC

**Approved/Pipeline Drugs:**
- Complement inhibitors (eculizumab)
- CXCR4 antagonists (plerixafor)
- CD40L antibodies (frexalimab)
- BAFF inhibitors (belimumab)
